Investor brief

The trust layer for everyday work.

An early-stage, openly documented marketplace for verified local work. Here is the thesis, what exists, what doesn't, and the conversations we want to have.

Open the pitch deck → Start a conversation

The thesis

Informal local work is enormous, recurring and almost entirely unbundled. Discovery happens by word of mouth, agreements live in chat, and settlement happens in cash. Each side carries the whole risk of the other side being unknown.

Job boards attacked discovery and stopped. They optimised for posting volume, not for whether the person showed up, the work was completed, or the payment was right. We think the durable business is the transaction layer — the thing both parties trust enough to settle through.

Why we can build it now

Smartphone penetration, hosted KYC as an API, and normalised digital settlement collapsed the cost of a trusted marketplace. The remaining work is product and operational, not fundamental infrastructure.

The wedge

Start with a narrow, high-frequency category and one geography where shift-based work repeats. Verification and a shared agreement create immediate value on the very first job; reputation and payment history make the second job cheaper and the tenth job nearly frictionless. Repetition is the moat — not the listing.

Business model

The natural model is a transparent service or transaction fee on settled shifts, with optional employer tooling (bulk shifts, invoicing, workforce analytics) as a later subscription layer. Exact pricing is a pilot question, not a deck assumption. Unit economics depend on repeat hiring frequency and settlement take-rate — both are the metrics we would design a pilot to measure.

Stage

Pre-seed · product built

  • Working Flutter web client in public beta
  • Live Node/TypeScript API + Supabase Postgres
  • Hosted KYC integration path designed and documented
  • Escrow-style ledger model with review and dispute states
  • Open architecture, security, privacy and QA documentation
Open risks — stated plainly
  • Licensed payment settlement not yet live
  • KYC coverage and consent review needed per launch market
  • Supply density and repeat-hire rate unproven at scale
  • Legal, labour and tax review outstanding
  • Mobile release and field-mode reliability still ahead
What we're looking for
  • Employer pilot partners with recurring shift demand
  • Identity, payment and insurance infrastructure partners
  • Early-stage investors with marketplace operational experience
  • Introductions to local regulators and labour experts
A note on numbers

This brief deliberately contains no invented traction metrics, market-size figures or fundraising targets. We will share measured pilot data as it exists. If you'd like the underlying plan, the startup kit in the repository lays out the model, assumptions, KPIs and risk register for review.
