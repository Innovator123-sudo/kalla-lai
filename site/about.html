About us

Work deserves better infrastructure.

Kalla-Lai is an early-stage product company building the trust layer for everyday, local work — starting in Nepal, starting with the shifts that never make it onto a job board.

The story

Most work in our communities is found through someone who knows someone. A phone call, a chat message, a cash payment at the end of the day. It is fast, human and deeply local — and it leaves everyone exposed. A worker has no proof of reliability beyond the last person who hired them. An employer has no way to check who is coming to their home or site. When something goes wrong, there is no record to point to.

We started Kalla-Lai because the tools that fixed this for software freelancers and ride-hailing never reached the everyday service economy. The ingredients now exist — a smartphone in every pocket, identity verification as an API, and digital payments people already trust. What was missing was a single layer that joins them into one understandable journey for both sides.

Our mission

Make trusted work accessible to everyone.

Every completed shift should leave both people better off — and leave behind a record that makes the next one easier, safer and faster.

How we build

Honesty over polish

The app never shows "verified", "paid" or "published" unless an authoritative service confirms it. A pretty screen that lies is worse than a plain one that tells the truth.

Privacy by design

Identity checks happen with a specialist provider. We keep a session reference, consent record and decision — not your documents, face templates or home address.

Open by default

Architecture, workflow, security, privacy and QA decisions are documented in the public repository. Trust is easier when the reasoning is visible.

Both sides, always

A marketplace that only serves one side breaks. Worker reputation and employer credibility are designed together.

Where we are

Kalla-Lai is an early-stage, product-first venture. There is a working web client, a live API, real database migrations and a documented integration path for identity verification. Regulated payment settlement, full KYC coverage for launch markets, legal review and mobile release are open items — see the roadmap and investor brief for the unvarnished version.
