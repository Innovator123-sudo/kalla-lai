Trust & safety

Trust is the product.

Not a badge, not a promise in a footer. Here are the actual controls built into the client, the API and the data model — and the ones still ahead of us.

Identity verification

A hosted KYC provider performs document OCR, selfie/face comparison and liveness checks. The user gives explicit consent, and approval arrives through an HMAC-signed webhook before the marketplace unlocks. A completed SDK session alone is never treated as proof.

What we do not store

No document images, no face templates, no OCR payloads, no home street addresses. The platform keeps a session reference, consent version/time and decision state. Profile setup asks for an area or city, not a precise location.

API integrity

Role-based authorization on every marketplace route, idempotency keys for state changes, structured validation, rate limiting and signed webhook verification. Access tokens are short-lived with rotating refresh sessions.

Money, honestly modelled

An escrow-style ledger separates agreed, approved and settled amounts and includes review and dispute states. Real settlement requires a licensed payment provider; the UI is built never to claim funds moved without confirmation.

Two-sided accountability

Worker reputation and employer credibility are both first-class. Rich profiles, work history and completion records give each side information before they agree to a shift.

Honest UI states

No "verified" badge without an approved decision. No "paid" label without a confirmed settlement. No "published" job without a persisted requisition. The interface is designed to fail visibly and safely.

Still to complete before production

Payment-provider settlement and payouts, per-market KYC and consent review, retention and deletion policies, legal and labour review, private object storage for profile media, and full release testing. These are tracked openly in the roadmap and the legal checklist.
