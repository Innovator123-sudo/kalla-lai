Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated: 2026 · Plain-language working draft

Draft for review — not legal advice

This is a plain-language working draft prepared for an early-stage product. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and is not yet a binding agreement. Have qualified counsel in your launch jurisdiction review it before the service accepts real users, payments or identity data.

1. Who we are

Kalla-Lai (कल्ला-लाई) is an early-stage two-sided work marketplace. This notice explains what the current product does with information. As the service matures, this document will be replaced with a formally reviewed policy naming the legal entity, data controller and a dedicated contact address.

2. What the app collects

  • Account data: email address, display name, role (worker or employer), and a hashed password credential or a Firebase identity token.
  • Profile data: skills, area or city, languages, bio, portfolio links, organisation details and optional profile media. We ask for an area, not a home street address.
  • Marketplace data: requisitions, applications, shift and attendance states, reviews, dispute records and ledger entries.
  • Location: if you ask the app to use your location, your device position is read once, at high accuracy, so nearby work can be sorted by distance. Public discovery responses round coordinates to roughly 100 m, and the street address of a job is only returned to the employer who posted it. There is no background or continuous location tracking.
  • Technical data: access and refresh tokens, request metadata needed for security and reliability, and locally stored preferences (theme, language, notification setting, API endpoint override).

3. Identity verification

Verification only runs after you give explicit consent. You send a photo of your identity document and a selfie over HTTPS. The check covers the document type, the text printed on the document (OCR), the name match, face comparison and liveness.

  • Not stored: the document photo, the selfie, and any face or biometric template. The images are used for the check and then discarded — they are not written to our database or to any object store.
  • Stored: the decision (verified or needs action), the document type, the match scores, the consent version and time, and the text fields read from the document, which can include your name, document number, date of birth and the address printed on the document.
  • If a hosted provider is configured to perform the check instead, Kalla-Lai keeps only the provider session reference, the consent record and the decision. That provider's own privacy notice governs its processing.

A verified badge or a verified account state is only reflected in the app after a signed confirmation of the decision. A completed check session on its own is never treated as proof.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not claim a worker is verified, a job is published or money has moved without an authoritative confirmation.
  • We do not keep your ID photos, your selfie, face templates or a home street address.
  • We do not track you continuously or in the background. Your position is read once, at high accuracy, only when you ask the app to use it.

5. Where data lives

The API uses a managed Postgres database (Supabase) and a hosted deployment. Locally stored preferences and session tokens are kept in your browser's storage so you stay signed in. Signing out clears the saved session on that device.

6. Retention and deletion

A formal retention schedule and a self-service deletion flow are open items on the roadmap. Until they are implemented, contact us through the contact page to request deletion of an account.

7. Your choices

  • Edit profile details from the app at any time.
  • Sign out to clear the saved session on the current device.
  • Choose theme, language and notification preferences in Settings.
  • Request access to or deletion of your data via the contact page.

8. Changes

This draft will change as the product and its legal review progress. Material changes will be highlighted in the repository and in the app.
