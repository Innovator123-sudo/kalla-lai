Roadmap

What exists, what's next, what's still an idea.

Published with real status labels. If a capability is not live, it isn't described as live.

● ShippedPhase 1

Client & core marketplace

  • Flutter web client with animated intro, worker/employer journey and settings
  • Email/password and Google sign-in with persisted sessions
  • Worker profile with skills, area, languages, portfolio and promo media
  • Employer profile with company info, categories, portfolio and contact details
  • Vacancy discovery, hiring requisitions, shift and escrow-ledger states
  • Admin people console with staff profile editing
● ShippedPhase 1

Backend & trust foundations

  • Node/TypeScript API with role-based access, idempotency and structured errors
  • Supabase Postgres with versioned migrations applied on startup
  • Hosted identity verification (document OCR, selfie, liveness) with HMAC-signed decision webhook
  • Privacy-by-design storage: decision state and consent record only, no identity images
  • Open documentation: architecture, workflows, data, API/realtime, security/privacy, UI/UX & QA
◐ In progressPhase 2

Production hardening

  • Licensed payment provider for real settlement and payouts
  • KYC coverage, consent versioning and retention/deletion policy per launch market
  • Production secrets, CORS allow-list, rate limiting and observability
  • End-to-end test coverage for verification, dispute and settlement paths
  • Employer pilot programme with measured repeat-hire and completion rates
○ PlannedPhase 3

Field-ready mobile

  • Android and iOS builds with native Google sign-in
  • Push notifications for dispatch, attendance and settlement events
  • Offline-tolerant field flows for low-connectivity shifts
  • Local-language accessibility review (नेपाली)
○ PlannedPhase 4

Scale the trust layer

  • Reputation scoring with transparent, contestable inputs
  • Dispute resolution SLAs and escalation tooling
  • Insurance or guarantee partnerships for high-value engagements
  • Employer tooling: bulk shifts, invoicing, workforce analytics
  • Multi-city operations and category expansion
Sequencing logic

Verification, a shared agreement and an auditable payment trail have to work together before volume is useful. We are deliberately not optimising for listing count until the trust loop closes end to end.

Work, verified. A trusted marketplace where independent workers and employers find each other, agree on the shift, and get paid with confidence.
