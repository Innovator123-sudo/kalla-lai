Terms of service

Last updated: 2026 · Plain-language working draft

Draft for review — not legal advice

This is a plain-language working draft prepared for an early-stage product. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and is not yet a binding agreement. Have qualified counsel in your launch jurisdiction review it before the service accepts real users, payments or identity data.

1. About this build

Kalla-Lai is an early-stage, publicly accessible product preview. It is provided to demonstrate and validate the marketplace workflow. It is not yet a production-grade employment, staffing or payment service, and it does not currently settle real money. Please treat all data entered as non-production data.

2. Using the service

  • Provide accurate information about yourself and the work you offer or request.
  • Use one account per person or organisation, and keep your credentials secure.
  • Do not use the service to impersonate someone, misrepresent verification status, post unlawful work, or harass other users.
  • Do not attempt to bypass access controls, probe the API for vulnerabilities without authorisation, or interfere with other users' data.

3. Roles and relationships

Kalla-Lai provides a marketplace and record-keeping layer. It is not an employer of workers, nor an agent of employers, and it does not currently act as a payment institution. Agreements for work are between the worker and the employer. Nothing in the app constitutes legal, employment, tax or financial advice.

4. Verification

Identity checks are performed by a third-party provider. A verification badge reflects a provider decision confirmed through a signed webhook; it is not a guarantee of a person's conduct, skills or suitability. Employers remain responsible for their own hiring decisions.

5. Payments and disputes

The current build models an escrow-style ledger and dispute states but does not hold, transfer or settle funds. Any payment arrangement shown in the preview is illustrative. Licensed settlement is a roadmap item and will be governed by separate terms with the relevant provider when launched.

6. Availability and changes

The preview may change, reset or become unavailable without notice. Demo and preview data may be removed. We aim to be transparent about the state of the product — see the roadmap.

7. Intellectual property

The client, API and documentation are published in the public repository. Refer to the repository for its licensing status before reusing any material. The Kalla-Lai name and logo remain the property of the project.

8. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, the preview is provided "as is" without warranties. Because this is an early-stage preview without production payments or regulated verification, users should not rely on it for decisions with financial or safety consequences.

9. Contact

Questions about these terms can be raised through the contact page or as an issue in the public repository.
