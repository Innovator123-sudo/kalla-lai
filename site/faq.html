FAQ

Straight answers.

Including the uncomfortable ones about what isn't finished yet.

A two-sided work marketplace for independent service providers and the people who hire them. It brings verification, discovery, shift coordination, reputation and payment tracking into one place instead of leaving them to phone calls and chat groups.

There is a working web app you can open from this site. It is an early-stage public build connected to a live backend; it is not yet a production-grade service with regulated payments or full KYC coverage. Expect rough edges.

Two groups: workers who want a steady, trustworthy way to find and be paid for shifts, and employers — households and businesses — who need verified help without the uncertainty of informal hiring.

A hosted identity provider performs document OCR, selfie comparison and liveness checks. Only a session reference, consent record and decision state are stored by the platform — not document images or biometric templates. Verification is only reflected in the app after a signed provider webhook confirms approval.

Not in the current build. The app models an escrow-style ledger and clear payment states; real settlement requires a licensed payment provider and is on the roadmap. The UI is deliberately designed never to claim money moved unless a provider confirms it.
